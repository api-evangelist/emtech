# EMTECH

> EMTECH is a financial-technology company (founded 2019) that modernizes financial market infrastructure for central banks, financial regulators, and financial service providers. Its Beyond Suite covers regulatory sandbox management, digital cash / CBDC infrastructure, automated compliance operations, and risk-based supervision, exposed through regulatory-reporting and data-ingestion APIs. Authentication is via per-organization API keys with separate Test and Live environments. EMTECH is SOC 2 and ISO 27001 certified and serves African central banks including the Bank of Ghana, the Central Bank of Nigeria, and Liberia.
